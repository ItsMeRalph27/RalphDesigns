03 / ABOUT & EXPERIENCE

From pixels to
production.

Bridging visual design with real-world production.

I’m a multidisciplinary Graphic Designer and creative developer with 6+ years of hands-on experience, turning ideas into polished, production-ready visual systems. My work connects branding, apparel, sportswear, full-dye sublimation, marketing graphics and digital interfaces — combining strong visual direction with the technical discipline required to make every design work beyond the screen.

My approach bridges visual creativity and real-world production. I create precise vector artwork, multi-panel garment layouts, sportswear graphics, mockups, campaign assets and responsive interfaces with careful control of typography, composition, color, file structure and print requirements. I design with the final output in mind, so the work is not only visually strong but also practical, scalable and ready to manufacture or publish.

I work across the complete creative pipeline — from concept development and art direction to vector construction, layout refinement, mockup presentation, production artwork, print preparation and digital implementation. My goal is to reduce the gap between concept and final output through clear ideas, organized files, accurate artwork and consistent execution.

Over the years, I’ve developed my process in fast-moving creative and production environments where deadlines, revisions, brand consistency and manufacturing requirements are part of the job. My experience covers sportswear and full-dye sublimation, apparel graphics, brand identity, promotional campaigns, automotive-inspired graphics, social content and web interfaces. I think beyond a single artwork — I build visual systems that remain consistent from the first concept through final production.
